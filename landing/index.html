Track the choices that shape your footprint

Everything in cli-MATE is built around a few daily actions that add up to real emissions — and real progress.

Transportation

Manual entry or GPS-assisted logging for walk, bike, bus, car, rideshare, and flight behavior.

Diet

Meal logging, barcode scanning, and future integration with nutrition apps for impact-aware food choices.

Shopping

Track purchases and patronage, flag fast fashion, and highlight local stores over chain-heavy habits.

Recycling

Simple manual check-ins for recycling and compost habits so the quiet wins actually count.

Make climate action feel social.

cli-MATE blends habit tracking, daily challenges, and friendly competition into one clean experience. The goal is accountability without guilt — more like a fitness app than a lecture.

Daily, weekly, and monthly challenges Keep momentum with lightweight goals and streaks.
Points and tiers Score choices against a carbon baseline and earn Bronze through Platinum.
Weekly leaderboards Compare with friends and celebrate the group’s total CO2 saved.

Built for friends, challenges, and shared momentum

Reach features for the next phase

Once the core behavior loop lands, cli-MATE can expand into maps, integrations, and smarter automation.

Eco store map

Find thrift shops, compost hubs, recycling drop-offs, and other climate-friendly places nearby.

Receipt parsing

Upload grocery receipts later for AI-assisted food impact detection and simpler logging.

App integrations

Connect rideshare and delivery apps to auto-log high-impact actions where possible.

Business ratings

Use crowdsourced sustainability scores for restaurants and stores people visit every day.
