# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People living in cities who want to understand and improve their climate impact without turning it into a guilt-heavy chore. They are comparing everyday choices, tracking progress over time, and sharing wins with friends.

## Product Purpose

cli-MATE makes sustainability visible, social, and easy to act on. It helps people log ordinary choices like transit, meals, shopping, and recycling, then turns that data into scores, streaks, and friend comparisons that make progress feel tangible.

## Brand Personality

Warm, social, practical.

The interface should feel optimistic and grounded, with the confidence of a well-made consumer product rather than a moral lecture. It should encourage action, celebrate small wins, and keep the tone friendly and clear.

## Anti-references

Avoid guilt-driven climate messaging, sterile SaaS dashboards, generic eco stock imagery, and anything that feels preachy or academic. Do not make it look like a corporate sustainability report, a dark-data analytics tool, or a gamified app overloaded with badges and noise.

## Design Principles

- Make climate action feel doable in daily life, not abstract.
- Keep social comparison lightweight and encouraging, never shaming.
- Surface progress quickly so the user understands the payoff immediately.
- Use clear hierarchy and concise copy to make logging feel low-friction.
- Let the landing page feel like a product people would want to join with friends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Keep motion subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences. Use inclusive language, avoid relying on color alone to communicate status, and design for mobile-first use in real-world, on-the-go conditions.
