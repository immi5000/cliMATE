# 5Locks Reference 01 — "Apps locked" hero screen

A reference screenshot from the **5Locks** prayer-focused app, provided as a moodboard. **We do not copy this verbatim** — cliMATE uses a cream canvas, not dark green. But the *pacing* and *type hierarchy* are exactly what to mirror.

---

## What's on screen

- Status bar at top (time, signal, battery — standard iOS).
- Top-left back button: "◀ App Store".
- Centered eyebrow text in tiny letter-spaced caps: `5LOCKS`.
- Huge serif headline left-aligned across two lines: *"5Locks blocks your apps at prayer time."*
- Lower 50% of screen: a phone-mockup illustration showing the in-app "Apps locked" state — three app icons (TikTok, lock, Instagram) with a glowing yellow lock in the center, "Apps locked" label below, "Pray to unlock your apps." subhead, and a yellow pill button labeled "Salah Complete".
- Below the illustration, a paragraph of supporting copy: *"Apps stay locked until you tap 'Salah Complete' — and every prayer you make is tracked."*
- White pill button at the bottom: "Continue".

## Background

Deep dark green canvas (~`#0F3D34`).

## Type hierarchy

- Eyebrow: ~10px caps, letter-spacing wide, off-white at 60% opacity.
- Headline: serif, ~44px, weight 700, white. Two lines, left-aligned.
- Mockup labels: sans-serif, white.
- Supporting copy: ~15px sans-serif, off-white at ~70% opacity, centered.
- Button label: 17px, dark text on white pill.

## Accents

- Gold/yellow (`#D9B85F` ish) — used for the lock icon and "Salah Complete" pill button.
- Otherwise monochrome.

## What to borrow for cliMATE

- **Pacing**: one big idea per screen, lots of empty space.
- **Eyebrow tag pattern**: tiny caps with wide letter-spacing above the headline.
- **Hero illustration in the lower half**: works well for Welcome and Results.
- **Single dominant CTA at the bottom**: pill button, full width inside margins.
- **Supporting copy below the hero, not crammed into it**.

## What to *not* borrow

- Dark canvas → we use cream (`#f2f0eb`).
- Serif headline → we use Manrope (sans-serif) throughout.
- Gold for chrome accents → we reserve gold (`#cba258`) only for big stat numbers on Reveals and Results, not for buttons or icons.
