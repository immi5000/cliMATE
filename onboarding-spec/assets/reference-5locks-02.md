# 5Locks Reference 02 — "Let's customize" warm-bubble cluster

A reference screenshot for the **Welcome / pre-onboarding intro** moment.

---

## What's on screen

- Same deep-green canvas.
- Top half (~40% of viewport): a soft cluster of overlapping translucent circles in warm tones — orange, yellow, teal — with one tiny circle floating above the cluster. The composition feels like a friendly abstract bubble bouquet.
- Below the illustration, centered:
  - Serif headline two lines: *"Let's customize 5Locks for you"* — with "5Locks" rendered in a gold tint.
  - Subhead in sans-serif: *"A few quick questions about your prayers and phone habits."*
- Bottom, centered: ghost text-button "Bismillah, let's get started →" (no pill — just a text link with an arrow).

## Color palette of the illustration

- Warm yellow `#E9C66B`
- Soft orange `#E89F62`
- Pale teal `#5FA295`
- Cream highlight `#F1E6C9`
- Each circle has alpha ~70% so they overlap into new colors.

## What to borrow for cliMATE

- **The bubble cluster as the Welcome hero illustration.** The cliMATE palette translation:
  - Warm cream `#f2f0eb` background.
  - Bubbles in: `#cba258` (cliMATE gold), `#d4e9e2` (green-light), `#00754a` (green-accent at ~50% opacity), `#1e3932` (house-green at ~30% opacity), plus one neutral cream-white bubble.
- **Headline with one word in gold** — for cliMATE, the word "footprint" in the Welcome headline could pick up the gold accent (optional polish, not required).
- **Subhead style**: warm, conversational, second-person.

## What to *not* borrow

- The "Bismillah" word — we adopted that label as a small homage in the Welcome CTA, but the rest of the spec is religion-neutral.
- The ghost text-button at bottom — cliMATE uses a solid pill CTA for clarity.
