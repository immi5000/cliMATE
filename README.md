# cli-MATE

**Compare your climate impact with friends.**

cli-MATE is a city-first phone app that turns everyday choices into visible climate impact. From commuting and meals to shopping, recycling, and local patronage, the app helps people track their footprint, earn points for better choices, and stay accountable together.

## The Big Idea

Most climate apps feel abstract. cli-MATE makes sustainability feel social, lightweight, and motivating — like a fitness app for your carbon habits.

- See your total CO2 emissions saved in real time
- Track daily impact across transportation, diet, shopping, and recycling
- Join friends in weekly leaderboards and friendly challenges
- Share progress with clean, story-ready score cards

## What cli-MATE Tracks

### Transportation

Log walks, bikes, buses, cars, rideshares, and flights manually — or detect movement with GPS when available.

### Diet

Add meals by hand, scan packaged food barcodes, or connect with meal apps later. Food choices can range from vegan-friendly to high-impact meals.

### Shopping & Patronage

Record what you buy and where you buy it. cli-MATE can flag fast fashion, chain-heavy habits, and more sustainable local alternatives.

### Recycling Practices

Track the habits that rarely get credit, like sorting waste properly and using compost or recycling systems consistently.

## How It Feels

cli-MATE is designed to motivate without guilt.

- Gentle notifications instead of shame-heavy alerts
- Streaks for habits like “7 days no car commute”
- Weekly scores that earn Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum tiers
- Personal goals like “go meat-free 4x this week”

## Social Layer

Sustainability gets easier when it’s shared.

- Add friends with a simple friend code
- Compare weekly scores on a leaderboard
- Start challenges like a 2-week no-beef run
- Share a collective impact feed showing what your group saved together

## App Flow

### 1. Onboarding

An opening logo fade-in leads into a quick intro explaining the app’s purpose: compare EcoScore with friends, track eco-friendly activity, and make sustainable choices feel rewarding.

Then the app collects lightweight profile details:

- First name
- Profile photo
- Location
- Profession
- Commute style and work type, including remote, office, or blue-collar / white-collar context

### 2. Main Dashboard

The dashboard opens with the total CO2 emissions saved, then surfaces:

- Daily and weekly challenges
- Current streaks and personal goals
- Recent activity logs
- Social progress against friends

### 3. Friends List

Users can browse friends, add new people with a code, and see who is leading the weekly impact race.

## Reach Features

These features extend the idea beyond MVP:

- Map of eco-friendly stores like thrift shops, compost hubs, and recycling points
- App integrations for ride and delivery services
- Receipt photo upload for AI-powered impact parsing
- Merchant sustainability ratings for local restaurants and stores

## Why It Works

cli-MATE makes climate action visible, measurable, and social. Instead of asking people to change everything at once, it turns daily routines into a game of better choices, friend accountability, and shared momentum.
